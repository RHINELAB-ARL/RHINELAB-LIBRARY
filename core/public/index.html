2 Introduction

This is the documentation page for many of the development of the Modelling, Theoretical Artificial Intelligence and Learning-theoretic Group (MTAIL) and documentation thereof. It will also include several notes and exposure of the lab itself here, including the ranking order framework itself (this one is under-developed, but sure, we might as well include the bare-bone), many notes and question debates, and more. First created by the necessity of purpose for reorganizing the research program into a multi-people central consensus rather than a solo-working environment, the website is minimally created to hook with the main website of the laboratory, and host all capable form of documents and writing relevant to the work that the lab group conducts itself of.

Currently, the list of inclusion, to-do works and many requests for migration of details, build-up and so on, are listed as followed. Note that some of them are still in settlement stage.

  1. Neural network formalism: This one has a website, however that is right now outdated and out of control, so it should be reorganized soon so we can start. Such is to realize of the capacity of the neural formalism using neural units to its full interpretation, not the narrow definition taken of virtue by normal scientific approaches of the minimal neuron.
  2. Foundational modelling theory in construction. We are still having to migrate the contents here, especially on encoding dependency and the formalization of gauge invariance formalism beside the age-old incomprehensible stack of explanations that serve no one but misunderstanding and implicit notions.
  3. Theoretical computer science, evolution theory, theoretical learning theory (computational learning theory, statistical learning theory and more), mathematical theories and epistemology, with certain aspects a few fields like iterative process, simulation theories and so on are in list of construction.
  4. Question sites and justification template. So for each question, asking such requires you to form certain line of thoughts: justification, logic of deliverance, and then unresolved tension and specification of what the question entails, or would it give more questions to come.

The goal would be long, and I will have to include them more in the future. But for now, I guess this is fairly enough. Further clarification would be delivered in their respective section instead, so it should worry not about said topic than the one held responsible, i.e. the group leader.

2.2 II. UNIG-1 - Supporting Information

Note

Since RHINE@MTAIL is dedicated as an UNIG (Универсальная научно-исследовательская группа, or universal scientific research group) for RHINELAB in total, this framework is to serve as lab-based evaluation on theoretical directive and dependency ledger tasking.

Temporary Framework: The rough ordering level

For certain purposes, the rather higher partition in the list contains another foundation group on the theoretical theory directive, which is the encompassing category of both the artificial intelligence group and the physics studies group right now. By category, we have the dependency or foundational order concept, which categorizes the following by order of C(X) for X count as the ascending level (I being the highest). By virtue of the position that modelling precedes axiom 3, the inversion invites the following ladder as to be true, in a restricted form, yet nevertheless appropriate of its propensity to be taken forward:

3 Of this, befitting of the goal thereof to be said, picking out structure, deciding what to represent, choosing what relationships matter - which represents the part of which modelling takes place, and of which such word gains its strength - is epistemically and ontologically prior to the formalization that axioms represent, nor the act of isolation. One can see the fact that idealization and of which universality, is the fact being compressed, of the messy world where one to controls it requires simplification, not the act to be disbanded and berated, but the delusion thereof to subsume of the reality it belongs toward.

  • Theory of modelling and the application thereof (CI).
  • Mathematical theory and encoding language research (CI).
  • Theory of computer science and automata realization (CII).
  • Practical computer instantiation consideration (CIII).
  • Physics theory and mathematical physics (CII).
  • Practical-theoretical physical gap (CIII).
  • Artificial intelligence theory (CII).
  • Artificial intelligence practical-theoretical implementation (CIII).
  • Hardware-interfaced-or-designed neural formalism network (CIII).

And so on, with more to add on the list, and more to relinquish such description to a dedicated section rather than passing words of sketchwork. This is also to separate dependency layer from which later on we will expand for experiment, real system or any amount of resources for practical purposes. I.e. we will also do practical stuffs, but have to retain the order of dependency. The reasoning would have to be seen elsewhere, as it would require a series of intensive illustration, justification and overall subjective view, opinionated analysis and henceforth, much to the description being rather long and tedious, within reason of helpfulness and completeness be rather treated carefully, or of the worst outcome be the invalidation of efforts taken to enforce such programme. Nevertheless, such is useful, and one should also take into account why modelling is stayed on top.
